# How to play Who Am I?

Rohosen's script, checked against the code: the category rail (`src/components/home/category-hero-rail.tsx`),
the generator (`src/lib/hooks/use-generator.ts`: three-second countdown, name stays until Regenerate),
the no-repeat shuffle bag (`src/lib/deck/shuffle-bag.ts`), the deck settings
(`src/components/generator/deck-settings.tsx`, `deck-filters.tsx`) and the Who Am I entry in `src/content/guides.ts`.
The spoken lines live in `dialogue.json`; this file is the readable version.

## Intro

Everyone at the table knows who you are. Except you.

## Setup

Grab your phones, open Who Am I, and pick a deck. Celebrity, Movies, Animals, Places, Objects, or Numbers. Tonight, we're doing Celebrity.

## Gameplay

Asha goes first. She generates a name, turns her phone sideways, and holds it up to her forehead. Ben and Kiko can see the screen. Asha can't.

Now she has to figure out who she is. She can ask anything, but the table can only answer yes or no. Am I alive? Am I an actor? Have I been in a Marvel movie?

You can keep asking as long as the answers are yes. Get a no, and it's the next person's turn. That's a house rule, by the way.

There's no timer, so take your time. The name stays on screen until you regenerate. And don't worry about getting the same person twice. The deck won't repeat anyone until you've gone through the whole thing.

## Decks and settings

Playing with kids? There's a Cartoon mode for that.

Got friends from outside India? You can switch India off in the settings.

Want Gandhi, Einstein, or other historical figures in the mix? There's a toggle for that too.

Movies come with their release year, just in case your memory of that one film is a little fuzzy.

Numbers is a surprisingly fun one. Pick a range, then start asking. Am I even? Am I bigger than fifty?

Places, Animals, and Objects work exactly the same way. Just a different kind of guessing.

## Winning

Once you've figured it out, tap regenerate and hand it to the next person. Or keep going. Nobody's stopping you.
